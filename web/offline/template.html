Where do I get a bill's XML?
  1. Go to govinfo.gov's Congressional Bills collection and find the bill (for example H.R. 4366, 118th Congress).
  2. Open each version you want to compare (for example "Reported in House" and "Engrossed in House") and download its XML.
  3. Pick the older file on the left and the newer one on the right.

PDFs can't be compared in this offline version yet. For PDFs, use the command-line tool.
